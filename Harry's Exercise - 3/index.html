Shubman Gill is an Indian international cricketer who was born on 8 September 1999 in Fazilka, Punjab, India. He is a right-handed top-order batter known for his elegant batting style and consistent performances. He has represented India in Test, ODI, and T20 international cricket. Shubman Gill has also played in the Indian Premier League (IPL) for Gujarat Titans, where he serves as captain. His major achievements include scoring a double century of 208 runs in an ODI against New Zealand in 2023. Through his dedication, hard work, and talent, he has become one of the well-known young cricketers in Indian cricket..
